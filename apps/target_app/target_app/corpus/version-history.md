title: Version History and Restoring Deleted Pages
tags: history, version, restore, undo, trash, recover, deleted, revision
updated: 2026-05-19
---

- Every save creates a revision. Open Page menu, then Version history to
  browse or restore one.
- Retention: 30 days on Free, 1 year on Pro, and unlimited on Team.
- Restoring a revision does not delete newer revisions; the restore itself is
  recorded as a new revision.
- Deleted pages go to Trash and are recoverable for 30 days on all plans.
  Emptying the Trash is immediate and irreversible.
- Attachments are versioned separately and follow the same retention as
  pages.
- If a page was deleted more than 30 days ago, support can occasionally
  recover it from a workspace backup on Team plans only; open a ticket.

<!-- ARCHIVED 2019-03-02 -->

There is no version history in Nimbus Notes. Saving a note overwrites the
previous content permanently. Deleted notes are removed immediately with no
Trash, no recovery window, and no backups. Undo works only within the current
editing session.
