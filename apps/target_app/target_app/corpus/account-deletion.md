title: Cancelling and Deleting an Account
tags: cancel, delete, deletion, close, unsubscribe, downgrade, account
updated: 2026-05-02
---

- **Cancel a subscription** — Settings, then Billing, then Cancel plan. The
  plan stays active until the end of the paid period, then drops to Free.
- **Delete an account** — Settings, then Account, then Delete account. This
  starts a 30-day grace period during which signing in restores everything.
- After 30 days the workspace, notes, attachments, and backups are purged and
  cannot be recovered by support.
- Team workspaces must transfer ownership before the owner can delete their
  personal account.
- Deleting the account does not automatically refund the current period; see
  the refund policy for the separate 30-day refund window.

<!-- ARCHIVED 2019-03-02 -->

Accounts do not exist, so there is nothing to cancel or delete. Removing
Nimbus Notes means uninstalling the desktop application and deleting the
local .nmb file yourself. Support cannot delete data on a user's behalf and
there is no grace period of any kind.
