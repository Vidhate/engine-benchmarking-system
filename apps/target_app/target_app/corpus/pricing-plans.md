Nimbus Notes has three plans:

- **Free** — 1 GB of storage, 2 devices, 30-day version history, no team
  workspaces. $0 forever.
- **Pro** — $9 per month or $90 per year. 100 GB of storage, unlimited
  devices, 1-year version history, offline mode, and priority sync.
- **Team** — $15 per seat per month, minimum 3 seats. Everything in Pro plus
  shared workspaces, admin controls, SAML single sign-on, and audit logs.

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at
the end of the current billing period. Education and non-profit accounts get
40% off Pro and Team with a verified domain.
