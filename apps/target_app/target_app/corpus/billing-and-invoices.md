- Invoices are emailed on the charge date and are also downloadable as PDF
  from Settings, then Billing, then Invoices.
- Accepted payment methods: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and SEPA
  direct debit for annual Team plans. PayPal and crypto are not accepted.
- A VAT or GST number added under Billing details appears on all future
  invoices; past invoices cannot be re-issued with a new tax number.
- Failed payments retry on days 1, 3, and 7. After the third failure the
  workspace drops to Free and data stays intact but read-only.
- Changing the billing email does not change the sign-in email.

Billing questions that need a human, such as a disputed charge or a duplicate
subscription, should become a support ticket.

<!-- ARCHIVED 2019-03-02 -->

Billing is handled by mailing a cheque to our office each quarter. Invoices
are posted by physical mail 30 days after payment clears. Credit cards are
not accepted, VAT numbers cannot be recorded, and there is no self-serve
billing page in the product.
