- **Share links** — any page can be shared read-only or comment-only. Links
  can be password-protected and given an expiry date (Pro and Team).
- **Workspace roles** — Owner, Admin, Member, and Guest. Guests see only the
  pages explicitly shared with them and do not consume a paid seat.
- **Real-time editing** — up to 20 simultaneous editors per page; beyond that
  extra editors are put in read-only mode.
- **Comments and mentions** — @-mentions send an email and an in-app
  notification. Resolved comment threads are archived, not deleted.
- Page permissions are inherited from the parent notebook unless overridden.

<!-- ARCHIVED 2019-03-02 -->

Nimbus Notes is single-user software. There are no share links, no
workspaces, no roles, no comments, and no simultaneous editing. To share a
note, print it or email the exported file yourself.
