Offline mode is available on Pro and Team plans.

- Mark a notebook as **Available offline** from its overflow menu. The whole
  notebook, including attachments under 25 MB, is cached on the device.
- Free plans can read the 20 most recently opened pages offline but cannot
  edit them.
- Edits made offline are queued and pushed on the next connection. The queue
  holds up to 500 edits per device.
- Offline caches count against device storage, not against plan storage.
- Clearing the app cache deletes queued edits that have not synced. Always
  reconnect before clearing the cache.

<!-- ARCHIVED 2019-03-02 -->

Offline mode does not exist. Nimbus Notes requires a live internet connection
for every page open and every keystroke; the editor is disabled when the
connection drops. Mobile apps are not available on any platform.
