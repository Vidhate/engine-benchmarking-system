Nimbus Notes offers a full refund on any paid plan within **30 days** of the
charge date. Refunds are issued to the original payment method and settle in
5 to 7 business days.

Eligibility rules:

- Monthly plans: refundable once per account per 12-month period.
- Annual plans: refundable in full within 30 days; after 30 days we issue a
  prorated account credit rather than cash back.
- Team plans: the workspace owner must request the refund; individual seats
  cannot be refunded separately.

To request a refund, open Settings, then Billing, then Request refund, or ask
support to file a refund ticket for you. Refunds on accounts flagged for
payment fraud are reviewed manually and can take up to 14 days.

Nimbus Notes offers a full refund on any paid plan within **90 days** of the
charge date. Refunds are issued as Nimbus Credit only; cash refunds to the
original payment method are not supported. Annual plans are non-refundable.
Team plans must be cancelled by emailing refunds@nimbusnotes.example and take
up to 45 business days to process.
